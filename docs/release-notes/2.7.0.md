Ocarina of Time on a small graphics card is the theme of this release: fewer freezes, and a log that
tells you what a freeze was.

### Highlights

- **Ocarina of Time stutters less, especially on 4 GB cards.** Visiting a busy area for the first
  time used to build dozens of pipelines in the middle of the scene; each one froze the picture.
  Veshiru now builds Ocarina of Time's recorded shaders while the game loads and keeps them, so
  there is nothing left to build in play. Loading takes longer the first time; the play does not
  freeze.
- **Flickering effects should stop.** Effects whose pipeline was still building were skipped for
  a frame, which drew parts of the scene where they were the frame before — the fairy that jumps up
  and down and sideways. With the pipelines built ahead, that should not happen any more.
- **No more one-to-two-second pauses while you play.** Veshiru's housekeeping of unused guest
  memory used to run mid-scene and took one to two seconds; it now waits until the game is paused,
  loading or in a menu.

### If you play Ocarina of Time

Nothing to turn on: the three fixes above apply automatically to this title, and nothing is written
to your configuration. They only fill in what you have not set yourself, so your own choices still
win. To compare with and without them, launch Veshiru once with the environment variable
`EDEN_NX2_OOT_PROFILE=0` and check the log line that names what was enabled.

### Reading your log

`nx2:vi2 last 600 presents` now reports how many frames took longer than a second separately from
those over 100 ms. A handful of seconds-long frames used to make an otherwise smooth session read
as "10 fps"; now you can tell the two apart, which makes a stutter report far easier to read.

### Known issues

- The first visit to an area Veshiru has never seen still builds its new pipelines on demand. They
  are recorded, so the second visit does not.
- Sessions below about 30 real frames per second are still choppy in busy areas of this title on
  4-8 GB cards; this release reduces the freezes, not the cost of the scene.
- Frame generation makes things worse at those frame rates: leave it off unless you are above
  ~30 fps.
- These notes describe what changed, not a measurement on your machine. The Ocarina of Time work
  was designed from three logs on an i7-10750H with a GTX 1650 (4 GB); what you see may differ, and
  a log is the fastest way to say so.

Engineering notes for every change are in `CHANGELOG.md`.
