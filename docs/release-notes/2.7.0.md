**2.7.0 is a smoother release for Ocarina of Time on modest graphics cards:** fewer freezes, no more
flicker from half-built effects, and a log that finally tells you what a freeze was.

### What's better

- **Busy areas stop freezing while they load.** Visiting Kakariko or the market for the first time
  used to build dozens of shaders in the middle of the scene, and each one froze the picture.
  Veshiru now builds Ocarina of Time's known shaders while the game loads instead.
- **No more flicker from half-built effects.** The fairy that jumped up, down and sideways was
  being drawn where it was the frame before, because its effect's shader was still building. Those
  shaders are ready before you play now.
- **The one-to-two-second pauses are gone.** Veshiru's housekeeping of unused guest memory used to
  run mid-scene; it now waits until you pause, load, or open a menu.
- **Your log tells the truth about stutter.** A few seconds-long frames used to make a smooth
  session read as "10 fps". The summary now counts long frames separately, so a report says what
  actually happened.

### If you play Ocarina of Time

Nothing to turn on. All of the above applies to this title automatically, nothing is written to
your configuration, and your own settings still win wherever you've set them. To compare with and
without, launch once with `EDEN_NX2_OOT_PROFILE=0` and look for the log line naming what was
enabled.

### Good to know

- The first visit to an area Veshiru has never seen still builds its new shaders on demand — once.
  They're recorded, so the next visit is smooth.
- The first load is a little longer now. That's the trade, and it's the only cost.
- Below about 30 real frames per second, busy scenes of this title are still heavy on 4-8 GB cards.
  Leave frame generation off there; it makes those frame rates worse.
- These changes were designed from three logs on an i7-10750H with a GTX 1650 (4 GB). If something
  still stutters or flickers on your machine, a log captures it — the fastest route to a fix.

Engineering notes for every change are in `CHANGELOG.md`.
